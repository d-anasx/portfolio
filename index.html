NeuroVision AI

An assistant for brain MRI analysis that sorts each scan into glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumour, or no tumour, and can point to where the tumour sits.

  • Input pipeline in tf.data: shuffling, prefetching and an 80/10/10 train, validation and test split on 224×224 scans.
  • Started with a CNN built from scratch, then moved to transfer learning and fine-tuning on MobileNetV2.
  • Trained a YOLOv8n detector to draw a box around the tumour region.
  • Every run tracked in MLflow; the best model is registered as the champion and served in a Streamlit app that clearly says it is not a diagnosis.

Machine learning, clustering and classification2026

CrediSegment

Credit card customers grouped by how they actually use their card, then a classifier that places any new customer in the right group.

  • Exploration first: skewness, outliers with IQR, Pearson correlations.
  • Preparation chain: log1p, StandardScaler, then PCA keeping the fewest components above 80% cumulative variance.
  • Compared K-Means and DBSCAN with the elbow method and silhouette score; the best setup was 2 components and k = 3.
  • Named the three segments (cash advance users, high activity, lower activity) and trained Logistic Regression, Random Forest and SVC with grid search, all logged in MLflow.
